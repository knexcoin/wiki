# KnexCoin Wiki

Welcome to the official KnexCoin documentation wiki.

**KnexCoin (KNEX)** is a next-generation blockchain ecosystem implementing **Proof-of-Bandwidth (PoB)** consensus combined with Proof-of-Stake. Designed to solve the blockchain trilemma, KnexCoin merges economic stake with actual bandwidth contribution to create a planetary-scale financial network.

## Core Principles

### Zero-Fee Transactions
Frictionless value transfer without intermediaries. Transactions are validated through bandwidth contribution rather than fee extraction.

### Proof-of-Bandwidth Consensus
Validators earn rewards for providing measurable bandwidth and uptime, creating real infrastructure value.

### Merchant-Driven Price Discovery
KnexCoin will **never** be listed on traditional exchanges. Price emerges organically from thousands of merchants setting their own KNEX acceptance rates.

### Fixed Supply
Hard cap of **210 million KNEX** with mathematically preserved scarcity through halving emissions.

## Ecosystem Components

### KnexWallet
Browser extension and PWA for managing KNEX accounts, supporting both custodial and self-custody modes.

### AyeDEX
Decentralized merchant exchange for organic price discovery through real commerce.

### NFC Integration
Physical card support for tap-to-pay KNEX transactions.

### Validator Network
Distributed network of bandwidth-providing validators securing the network.
